From the chairp. 02

Skratch Studio is a barbershop in Vilnius built on a single idea: a haircut is a ritual, not an errand. We don't rush the clippers and we don't do template cuts — every head gets read before a single line is set.

The room is black, chrome and quiet. You sit, we talk, and then the work begins — sharp fades, sculpted beards, the kind of finish that still looks right on day ten. This is the first issue of who we are and what happens in the chair.

Walk in sharp. Leave sharper. Everything after this page is the menu, the method and the people who make it.

"A good cut isn't loud. It just holds its shape when everything else moves."

— Skratch Studio, head barber
