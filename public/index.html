The Craft

Skratch Studio started with one chair and one rule: every cut earns its finish. We're a Vilnius barbershop built on precision, patience and a little attitude — no rushed clippers, no template cuts.

Skratch Signature

The room is black, chrome and quiet. You sit, we talk, then the work begins — and what you leave with still looks right on day ten.
